Tiny brain rally

Two cars, two brains, one track, training in parallel. The grip car is AWD on street tyres; the drift car is RWD on slicks, so throttle breaks the rear loose. Two stages, each mixing fast asphalt, loose gravel and rough ground in different proportions; white ticks on the road mark surface changes, and the brains sense the grip underfoot and ahead. Both cars have a footbrake they must learn to use; the drift car also has a handbrake. Each car works in two phases: it explores until it completes a lap, then locks a protocol (printed as a formula) and optimises lap time with faster tests: cars behind the record pace are cut early. Logs, records and lap telemetry export as files.
